Age 10 summer Part 38. Primary verse text and immediate context checked. This is a limited editorial check; independent Arabic and religious-context review remains pending.

  • W00940-M01 27:25: خبء is hidden matter that Allah brings forth and knows; a sealed envelope is only a limited language example, with no human claim to hidden knowledge. Quran.com verse or tafsir
  • W00948-M01 2:234: خبير describes Allah in the closing of a passage about widows and their decisions; do not turn this into family questioning or claim a reader knows a stranger’s circumstances. Quran.com verse or tafsir
  • W00985-M01 6:63: خفية describes supplication to Allah quietly in distress on land or sea; a private question box is a separate ordinary use of secrecy. Quran.com verse or tafsir
  • W00991-M01 2:254: خلة is friendship whose help is unavailable on the Day mentioned; the verse does not forbid ordinary friendship in this life. Quran.com verse or tafsir
  • W00997-M01 26:137: خلق الأولين is understood in the recorded sense as inherited custom in the rejecting people’s reply to Hud; it is not praise for every inherited practice. Quran.com verse or tafsir
  • W00997-M02 68:4: خلق describes the Prophet’s great character; this sense is distinct from inherited custom and is not a rating of a child. Quran.com verse or tafsir

Open source issue: بَرْد 21:69

The workbook definition records “شيء يريح الجسم من شدة الحرارة، وقيل هو النوم هنا” for بَرْد and cites 21:69. The verse says the fire was made cool and safe for Ibrahim; “sleep here” appears out of place with that witness. The workbook row is unchanged.

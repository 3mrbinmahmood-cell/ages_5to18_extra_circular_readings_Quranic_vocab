Open source issue: ثَلَاث 7:142

The workbook defines ثَلَاث as “العدد الذي يلي اثنين” and cites 7:142. The recorded verse uses «ثلاثين» for thirty nights. This may be a numeral-family citation rather than a direct example of the headword; specialist clarification is needed. The workbook row is unchanged, and W00647-M01 remains pending with no summer review credit.

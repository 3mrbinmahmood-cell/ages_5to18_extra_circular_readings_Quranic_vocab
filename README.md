# Qur’anic Vocabulary Reading Curriculum (Ages 5–18)

This repository contains an offline-readable Arabic reading curriculum with matching student and teacher HTML editions. Start at [index.html](index.html), which introduces the use case and links each age to its published period editions.

## Scope

The source inventory contains **5,311 separately recorded vocabulary meanings across 4,866 distinct source entries**: **1,585 verbs (فعل), 3,622 nouns (اسم / ism), 90 particles (حرف / harf), and 14 disjoint letters (حروف مقطعة)**. A meaning is a separately recorded sense, so the number of meanings exceeds the number of entries. The source workbook was used to build the inventory and is not redistributed here.

The [HTML coverage ledger](VOCABULARY_COVERAGE_LEDGER.html) lists every meaning and its planned student-page anchor. The [progress chart](VOCABULARY_PROGRESS_BY_AGE.html) shows age and period allocations, and the [curriculum map](curriculum_map.html) links available age/period editions.

## Age and grade navigation

The index pairs ages 7–18 with Grades 1–12, so **age 18 is shown with Grade 12**, as requested. Ages 5 and 6 are shown as early learning / Pre-K and Kindergarten. These are navigation labels, not a claim that school systems use the same age cutoffs.

## How the curriculum was assembled

1. The supplied vocabulary workbook was inventoried by distinct entry and separately recorded meaning; each meaning keeps a stable sense ID and source witness reference.
2. Meanings were allocated across age bands using a non-linear progression, then grouped into terms, seasonal readings, and complete lessons.
3. Student readings place meanings in Arabic Fusha stories, conversations, and practical contexts. Teacher editions pair the readings with guidance, suggested answers, activities, review prompts, contextual verb work, and learner adjustments.
4. The coverage ledger links each meaning to its planned student-edition location. The links were checked against the corresponding HTML anchors.
5. Age 11 was rebalanced across complete lesson units: 296 first-term meanings, 80 winter-reading meanings, 304 second-term meanings, and 70 summer-reading meanings (750 total).
6. The home page, map, and progress chart provide navigation and summary views. The pages use local HTML and a local stylesheet for offline reading.
7. Winter and summer review books were added for **ages 9, 12 and 14 (Grades 3, 6 and 8)**. Each season has six substantial readings and matching student/teacher editions: six books, twelve editions and 36 different readings altogether. Winter draws on first-term meanings; summer selects meanings from both terms. These are selective review books, not a second claim of new vocabulary coverage.
8. The [seasonal review ledger](SEASONAL_REVIEW_LEDGER.html) records the stable source meaning IDs selected in those readings, with links to both the original student presentation and the new review lesson. Delayed review and next-year retrieval prompts are supplied, while completion remains a learner observation rather than a writing claim.

The readings provide connected Arabic texts, conversations, comprehension and retrieval exercises, contextual verb work, practical tasks, spaced review, suggested answers and adjustments for learners who need support or additional challenge. Student and teacher reading text is checked for agreement. Local file links and lesson anchors are checked before publication. Arabic and Qur’anic specialist review remains pending.

## Editorial status and limits

The ledger records drafted curriculum content and intended presentation locations. **It does not prove that a teacher delivered a lesson or that a learner mastered a meaning.** Specialist Arabic and Qur’anic editorial review is pending. The source references and contextual uses should be checked by qualified reviewers before classroom adoption. The age/grade pairing is a user-requested navigation convention and should be adjusted for local school systems. The map identifies age/period combinations that do not yet have separate reading books; zero new meanings does not itself mean a review book exists.

## Files included

The release contains HTML curriculum and navigation pages, the local CSS stylesheet required by the editions, and this README. PDF editions, spreadsheets, source workbooks, and production scripts are excluded.
